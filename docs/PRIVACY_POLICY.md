## 1. Scope
This policy covers the Shekar Eye Hospital mobile app, the staff portal and related services (the "Services").

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account:** mobile number; name and email if you provide them.
- **Patient profiles** for you and family members you add: name, date of birth, gender, relation, medical history, allergies, whether you wear glasses.
- **Clinical information** created by the hospital: eye examination results, glasses prescriptions, medicines, diagnoses, reports and scans.
- **Appointments and payments:** bookings, visit reasons, payment amount and status, receipt numbers. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment processor, Razorpay. We do not receive or store them.
- **Symptom checker and assistant:** text you type, used to generate guidance.
- **Technical:** push-notification token, app version, and IP address in security logs.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes)
To register you and verify your phone number; book, manage and remind you of appointments; maintain your medical records; give you access to your reports; process payments and refunds; respond to your questions; keep the Services secure; and meet our legal and regulatory duties.

## 4. Consent
We process your personal data with your consent, given when you set up your profile, and where the law otherwise permits (for example, medical emergencies and legal record-keeping). You can withdraw consent at any time from Profile → Delete account, or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it, and some records must be kept by law (section 7).

## 5. Children and family members
A parent or legal guardian may add a child's profile. By doing so they confirm they are the child's lawful guardian and consent to processing the child's data for their care. We do not track children's behaviour or show them targeted ads.

## 6. Who we share it with
- Hospital doctors and authorised staff involved in your care (access is role-based and logged).
- Service providers acting on our behalf: hosting ([provider, India region]), SMS ([MSG91]), payments ([Razorpay]), push notifications (Expo, Google Firebase, Apple), and, if enabled, AI text generation for the help assistant ([Anthropic]).
- Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

## 7. Retention
Medical records are kept for as long as Indian law and medical-council guidance require [e.g. at least 3 years from the last visit, longer for minors or medico-legal cases; confirm with counsel]. When you delete your account, we delete your login and contact details and anonymise your account, but retain clinical records for this period. Security logs are kept for [1 year].

## 8. Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access requires one-time-password login, and staff permissions are role-based. Every access to records is logged. Servers are hosted in [India], and backups are encrypted. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights
You can access and correct your information in the app, ask for a summary of the data we hold, request erasure (subject to section 7), nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated, and raise a grievance with our contact above. We will respond within [the period set by the DPDP Rules]. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 10. Medical disclaimer
The symptom checker and help assistant give general information only. They are not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for a doctor. In an emergency, call [emergency number] or go to the nearest emergency department.

We will notify you in the app of material changes to this policy.
